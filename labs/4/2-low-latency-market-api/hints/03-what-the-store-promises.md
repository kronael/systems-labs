# What the store promises, and what you may not lean on

A question your `ARCHITECTURE.md` must answer: which of the store's
durability, eviction and availability properties the design relies on, and
which it explicitly does not.

It sits here rather than in the task because naming those properties points at
where the design puts its trust. Read the store's own documentation on
persistence and on eviction before you answer, and answer it for the
configuration this lab actually runs, not the one the documentation defaults
to.
