## Release v0.1.0

Phases 1 and 2 cleared both reviewers and the repository got its first commit.

Five review rounds. The first three failed, each finding real defects, and
several of those defects were introduced by the previous round's repair — the
claim that a relational store behind functions is a shape nobody deploys was
written while fixing an earlier finding, and the two-belief problem in `1/2`
came from adding the non-overlap invariant on top of an existing notification
lesson. Rounds four and five cleared.

The two deadlocked questions were settled rather than argued. An unbuilt
barrier signal does not block publishing a specification in a repository that
ships no code, and compiled fault schedules are sufficient because absolute
secrecy was never available on a machine the learner owns. A measured probe
settled the freeze mechanism itself: work stops while frozen and resumes on
thaw.

The best catch came last and was the oldest defect in the tree. Every brief
disclaimed its mechanisms — "the prompt does not prescribe a worker pool,
queue, semaphore" — and `CLAUDE.md` instructed exactly that. Naming a mechanism
to exclude it still names it. All ten briefs now state the categories of
decision the learner owns and name no candidate, and the lint fails a mechanism
name whether prescribed, disclaimed, or merely mentioned.

Tagged `v0.1.0`, detached HEAD, nothing pushed. The master spec stays `draft`,
so implementation remains gated on the owner.
