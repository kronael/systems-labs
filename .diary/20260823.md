## 12:00 2026-08-23

Labs lose their worked solutions entirely. A challenge has one correct answer,
so a golden reference is well defined; a systems lab admits many correct
designs, so none is canonical and "rotten" is one of countless ways to be wrong.
`golden/` was never load-bearing here as it is in `challenges/`, where
`make bench` runs it as the oracle for expected output — this checking asserts
invariants over observed histories and needs no oracle. The cited source
documenting the real reported behaviour becomes the lab's ground truth.
`template/` keeps its implementation as the scaffold's regression test.

Then the grader itself went. Thirty-one graders is more work than the curriculum
it grades, and learners will use agents to build and to check either way.
