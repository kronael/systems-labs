## 07:00

Ran four read-only lenses over the repository at once: technical adoption,
the teaching boundary, the learner's own experience, and scope. Each wrote to
`.ship/critique-<lens>-20260921.md`. I re-verified every citation against
disk before using any of it; three of the four produced findings I could
reproduce exactly, and none of the four overclaimed.

Two lenses reached the same conclusion from different directions, which is
the result I trust most today. The clock layer the fault contract requires
cannot be built: `time_namespaces(7)` does not virtualize `CLOCK_REALTIME`,
so a container has no wall clock of its own, and `libfaketime` cannot preload
into a static Go binary. `1/1` is the sharp end — it steps the clock between
"the two readings" of a comparison, its `README.md` never mentions a clock,
and a correct design reads the wall clock once. `HOWTO.md` sends every
learner to lab 01 first, so that is the first `make fault` anyone runs.
